# Cameron County coverage

The November 3, 2026 catalog contains 86 distinct contests across county offices, courts, school boards, cities, special districts, measures, state offices and federal offices. This is the union of ballot styles, not a claim that every county voter can vote in every contest. District selection remains manual.

Candidate names, reported parties and ballot choices were extracted from the official [county sample ballot collection](https://www.cameroncountytx.gov/elections/index.php/sample-ballots-2/), specifically the [396-page master ballot](https://www.cameroncountytx.gov/elections/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/26GCAMER_SAMPLE.pdf) and the separately listed [San Benito Municipal Utility District No. 1 ballot](https://www.cameroncountytx.gov/elections/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/San-Benito-Municipal-Utility-District-No.-1-Confirmation-and-Directors-Election-09182026.pdf). Verified November 2, 2026. Each catalog entry retains its ballot-page provenance.

The catalog includes Brownsville propositions A–O, the MUD confirmation proposition and three-seat director contest, 15 school board positions, two mayoral contests, and county-specific returns for state and federal races. Nonpartisan candidates remain neutral. Uncontested candidates are not automatically called winners.

All county entries are scoped to Cameron County voters. The same statewide or congressional contest elsewhere in the app has its own full-election totals; these cannot fill county cells.

The results adapter discovers official county PDF links only within the November 3, 2026 election section. It rejects historical elections, sample ballots, turnout documents and untrusted hosts. Supported text-native summary reports must identify the correct election, office and total-vote columns. Ambiguous proposition names require jurisdiction context. Missing values remain unknown, and prior successful counts are retained as delayed if refresh fails.

As of verification, the official results page rejects server requests and no November election-night summary has been verified. Live importing is therefore unverified until a supported report is published and accessible. Scanned PDFs, precinct-only reports or changed column formats require an adapter update. The app does not estimate reporting completion or forecast winners from count totals.
